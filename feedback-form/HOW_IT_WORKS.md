# 📧 How Email Notifications Work

## ✅ YES - Your Form Sends Responses to Your Email!

**Your email:** jajichandrakala@gmail.com

## How It Works (Like Google Forms):

1. **User fills out the form** → Submits feedback
2. **Form validates** → Checks all required fields
3. **Email is sent automatically** → To your email address
4. **You receive notification** → Just like Google Forms!

## What You'll Receive in Your Email:

Each time someone submits the form, you'll get an email with:

- ✅ **Name** - Who submitted the feedback
- ✅ **Email** - Their email address (you can reply directly)
- ✅ **Category** - Type of feedback (General, Bug Report, etc.)
- ✅ **Rating** - Their rating (1-5 stars)
- ✅ **Message** - Their complete feedback message
- ✅ **Timestamp** - When it was submitted

## Example Email You'll Receive:

```
📋 NEW FEEDBACK FORM RESPONSE

👤 Name: John Doe

⭐ Rating: 5/5

I love the new design! It would be great if you could add dark mode.
```
